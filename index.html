About

Honest painting & home touch-ups

V.C Painting LLC is a local painting and home-services business focused on doing the job right. Inside, we specialize in clean, careful interior painting. Outside, we cover the details that keep a home looking sharp — repainting and touching up exterior trim, refinishing deck support beams, pressure washing, and bringing tired outdoor furniture back to life.

Every job starts with a free, no-pressure estimate. We show up on time, protect your space, and clean up when we're done. Tell us what you need and we'll let you know honestly whether it's something we can help with.
